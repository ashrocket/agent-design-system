# Code of Conduct

We want a practical, welcoming project where disagreement produces clearer
formats and better tests. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss the work
rather than the person, and do not harass or discriminate against contributors.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that undermines that standard.
Report conduct concerns privately to the security/contact address published by
the repository owner. Until a public address is configured, do not include
sensitive reports in a public issue.
